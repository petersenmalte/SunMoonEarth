Notes on This Visualisation

  • Sizes and distances are schematic, not to scale.
  • Directions, angles and lighting follow the calculated values, not the shortened distances.
  • Moon phases arise from the Sun illuminating the lunar sphere, not from Earth's shadow.
  • Altitude and azimuth include the atmospheric refraction correction recommended by the library.
  • “Above” and “below the horizon” refer to the centre of the body. Rise and set times additionally account for the disc's edge and therefore fall a little later or earlier.

All astronomical positions, the Moon phase and illumination are calculated by Astronomy Engine, an open-source library implementing VSOP87 planetary theory for the Sun/Earth and an ELP2000-derived lunar theory for the Moon. 3D rendering: Three.js. Time zones and daylight saving: Temporal.
